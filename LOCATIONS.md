# Adult Lacrosse Locations — Massachusetts & New Hampshire

Research compiled from web/Brave search + direct site fetches. Details (schedules, prices, active/inactive status) change season to season — confirm directly before showing up. Every entry now includes a **Fields:** line noting Indoor, Outdoor, or Both, based on facility descriptions found via Brave search.
